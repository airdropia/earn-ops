# The Daejin thread, drafted before the trade

The bounty scores clarity 25 and hands on depth 25 out of 100, and the reach
and engagement rate are the other 30. So the thread has to teach a newcomer
how a shared pool market works, and then show a real position and a real
market. It must be original, published after the listing went live, kept
public, and it must not contain the wallet address, because the address is
collected with the submission.

Every post is written to stay under 265 characters, because the reply prefix
counts towards the limit. The six posts are meant to be posted as a reply
chain from the first one.

Placeholders to fill once the trade and the market exist:

- `[MARKET]` the featured market name, exactly as the app shows it
- `[SIDE]` Yes or No

- `[THESIS]` one or two sentences of real reasoning

- screenshots of the position and of the created market

## Post 1

Prediction markets look like betting apps. They are not. A shared pool
market has no bookmaker and no odds set in a back office. The price you see
is the crowd's probability, updated every second. Here is how it actually
works. 🧵

## Post 2

The pool: every trade puts USDC on one side. If the YES side holds 70% of the
pool, then $1 on YES buys the 70% share of the whole pool. Buy NO and you
own the other 30%. Your position is a share of the pool, not a ticket
against a fixed price.

## Post 3

Why the price moves: the pool is priced by the share ratio, nothing else.
More money lands on one side, that side's share gets more expensive, and the
price rises. So the price is the crowd's belief, expressed in a number you
can trade against.

## Post 4

How winners get paid: when the event resolves, the losing side's pool is
split between the people on the winning side, in proportion to their
shares. Hold 10% of the winning side and you get 10% of everything the
losing side put in. No house margin, no spread.
